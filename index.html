FAQ

Does FreelanceKit process client payments?

You can record payments you receive (UPI, bank transfer, cash, card). FreelanceKit does not currently process those client payments unless you connect a gateway later.

Where is my data stored?

Account data is stored in your Supabase project (cloud database) with row-level security so other users cannot read your records.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Free and Pro users can export clients, invoices, quotations, payments, and expenses.

Is this financial or legal advice?

No. FreelanceKit is productivity and invoicing software. Confirm tax treatment with a qualified professional.

Privacy & security

Authentication uses Supabase Auth. Passwords are never stored in FreelanceKit tables. Private pages are not indexed. Plan status is read from the database, not from values you could set in the browser.
